Privacy notice

Effective date: October 23, 2026

California Fish Stocking Tracker is an independent, unofficial browser extension. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share personal information through the extension.

Information stored in Firefox

The extension stores favorite water IDs and a temporary copy of public stocking records using Firefox extension storage. This data stays in the user's local Firefox profile and is not sent to the developer. Removing a favorite removes it from the saved favorites. Firefox normally removes extension storage when the extension is uninstalled.

Network connections

Opening or refreshing the popup requests public stocking information from the California Department of Fish and Wildlife schedule and its ArcGIS data service. The extension omits browser credentials and does not include the user's search text or favorites in those requests. Like other internet services, those providers may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and browser user agent.

Google Maps is contacted only after the user selects an “Open in Google Maps” link. The link includes the public coordinates of the selected water body. Google handles that visit under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

The extension does not create, read, or use cookies. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or user accounts.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
