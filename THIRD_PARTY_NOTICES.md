# Third-Party Data Notices

## California Department of Fish and Wildlife planting locations

The extension uses the following public dataset:

- Dataset: **Planting Location - CDFW [ds2897]**
- Publisher: California Department of Fish and Wildlife
- Official catalog: <https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/planting-location-cdfw-ds2897>
- ArcGIS service: <https://services2.arcgis.com/Uq9r85Potqm3MfRV/ArcGIS/rest/services/biosds2897_fmu/FeatureServer/0>
- License: [CC BY](https://opendefinition.org/licenses/cc-by/), as identified
  by the official catalog

The extension filters, normalizes, combines, and displays selected dataset
fields. These presentation changes are made by this project, not by CDFW.

## California Department of Fish and Wildlife planting schedule

Current and future announcements come from CDFW's public Fish Planting
Schedule:

<https://www.nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FishPlants/PublicPlantSearch>

CDFW states that schedules can change because of road, water, weather, and
operational conditions. The extension therefore displays planting weeks rather
than claiming an exact stocking day.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
California Department of Fish and Wildlife or the State of California.
